# Module 17 — Platform Engineering

> **Instead of every team doing DevOps themselves, Platform Engineering teams build the golden path — opinionated, self-service infrastructure that makes the right thing the easy thing.**

**Status:** This module unlocks after you complete through Module 12.

## What You'll Learn

- [ ] What Platform Engineering is and how it differs from DevOps
- [ ] **Internal Developer Platforms (IDPs)** — what they are and what they need to do
- [ ] **Backstage** — Spotify's open-source IDP (now a CNCF project)
- [ ] Software catalogs — central registry of all services, their owners, and their docs
- [ ] Golden paths — opinionated, pre-built scaffolding for new services
- [ ] **Crossplane** — Kubernetes-native infrastructure provisioning (like Terraform but K8s-native)

---

## Key Concepts

| Term | What it is |
|------|-----------|
| **IDP** | Internal Developer Platform — the tools and workflows a platform team builds for product teams |
| **Golden Path** | The opinionated, pre-built template for starting a new service (includes CI, monitoring, security out of the box) |
| **Software Catalog** | A searchable registry of every service: what it does, who owns it, where its docs are, its health status |
| **Self-service** | Product teams provision what they need without opening a ticket to the infra team |
